Recruiter outreach pipeline · how it works

Mino's solution, visualized

Recruiters send outreach from their own Gmail mailbox. Postgres decides who sends what, when. The worker just delivers.

12 tables OAuth in Vault RLS everywhere 5-min cron

What each piece does

📝

The campaign

Subject line, preview text, HTML body, send time, and the recruiter it is sent as. Stored in campaigns.

👥

The candidate list

Candidates imported from your ATS, sourcing tool or a CSV. Deduped by email, so one person is one row.

🔗

The recipient list

One row per (campaign, candidate). Tracks where each candidate is in the journey — pending → sent.

🔐

The mailbox keys

Each recruiter's Google OAuth tokens, encrypted in Supabase Vault. The database only stores the locker number, not the key.

🌡️

The reputation tracker

Brand-new mailboxes are throttled and loosen as they send. Recruiters on the same company domain share one budget, so one recruiter can't flag the whole team's domain.

📒

The audit ledger

Every send attempt — successful, failed, or lost — has a permanent row. Nothing is ever silently dropped.

How the tables relate

The dotted lines are Vault secrets. Everything else is a normal foreign key.

What happens to a single recipient

Each candidate in a campaign moves through these states. The lease token is what stops anyone from sending twice.

  • pending
  • leased (in flight)
  • sent
  • retry_scheduled
  • failed
  • send_unknown
stateDiagram-v2
  direction LR
  [*] --> pending : added to campaign

  pending --> leased : worker claims it
  retry_scheduled --> leased : retry time reached

  leased --> sent : Gmail returned OK
  leased --> failed : permanent error, or 5th attempt
  leased --> retry_scheduled : transient error
  leased --> send_unknown : lease expired or unclear
  send_unknown --> sent : late confirmation arrives
  send_unknown --> failed : late failure arrives

  pending --> canceled : unsubscribed or campaign canceled

  sent --> [*]
  failed --> [*]
  canceled --> [*]

One trip through the pipeline

A single candidate, from "added" to "delivered" — who talks to whom.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant U as Recruiter
  participant DB as Postgres
  participant W as Cron worker
  participant V as Vault
  participant G as Gmail
  participant P as Candidate inbox

  U->>DB: Create campaign + attach candidates
  U->>DB: dispatcher_campaign_go_live()
  Note over DB: Drops unsubscribed candidates,
checks at least 1 recipient

  loop every 5 min
    W->>DB: dispatcher_mark_expired_sends_unknown()
    W->>DB: dispatcher_claim_next_send_token()
    DB-->>W: Lease token + full email payload
    W->>V: Read OAuth tokens (via SECURITY DEFINER)
    V-->>W: Decrypted access token
    W->>G: users.messages.send
    G-->>W: 200 OK + messageId

    alt success
      W->>DB: dispatcher_receipt_sent(lease, messageId)
      Note over DB: status → sent
    else transient error
      W->>DB: dispatcher_receipt_failed(lease, retry_after)
      Note over DB: status → retry_scheduled
    else timeout or 5xx
      W->>DB: dispatcher_receipt_unknown(lease)
      Note over DB: status → send_unknown
    end
  end

  G->>P: Email delivered
  Note over DB: Trigger: no rows left in flight → campaign completed

Why brand-new senders are throttled

Gmail penalises mailboxes that suddenly start blasting. The policy ramps up gradually so reputation builds, not breaks.

~ 1 every 10 min
60

~ 1 every 5 min

~ 1 every 4 min
480

~ 1 every 3 min

Numbers are the rolling 24-hour cap per reputation scope. Recruiters on the same company domain share one budget; a recruiter on a personal gmail.com mailbox has their own.

Three guarantees the database makes

01

🔒 No double-sends

The lease token is unique per attempt. A receipt only counts if it matches the live lease — late or duplicate worker callbacks just bounce off. A candidate never gets the same email twice.

02

🌡️ No mailbox abuse

Postgres won't issue a token if the scope's 24-hour cap is hit or the recruiter's last send was too recent. Pacing is enforced centrally, across every worker and every recruiter on the team.

03

📒 Nothing gets lost

Worker crashes? Lease expires, candidate drops to send_unknown. A late receipt can still resolve it. Worst case: an operator sees it in the unknown_sends view.

What each table does

The gist. What it tracks, why it exists.

Authoring — what to send, and to whom

senders authoring One row per recruiter who sends outreach.
email & display name
Who the candidate sees in the From line.
disabled_at
Set when a recruiter leaves the team; their campaigns stop being claimed.
campaigns authoring The email itself. Subject, body, which recruiter sends it, when it should go out.
name
Internal label. Not shown to candidates.
subject & preview text
What candidates see in their inbox. {{first_name}} is filled per candidate.
body
The actual email content, with {{first_name}} and {{unsubscribe_url}}.
status
Draft → live → completed, with paused and canceled on the side. Only "live" campaigns are picked up by the worker.
send_at
When sending is allowed to start. Lets you schedule.
sender_id
The recruiter whose Gmail will be used to send it.
contacts authoring The candidate book, filled from your ATS, sourcing tool or CSV imports.
email
The address. Deduped by normalized email — one row per candidate.
first & last name
Used to personalise the email.
metadata
Anything else you want to keep, like the ATS id or the role they were sourced for.
campaign_contacts authoring Which candidates are getting which campaign. One row per (campaign, candidate). Each row tracks its own send journey.
status
Where this candidate is right now: pending, in flight, sent, failed, retry pending, unknown, or canceled.
send_after / next_retry_at
"Don't try this one again until …" — for scheduling and retries.
attempt_count
How many tries so far. The fifth retryable failure is final.
lease info
Marks a candidate as "currently being sent by a worker, hands off". Prevents double-sends.
sent_at & message id
Proof of delivery once Gmail confirms.
last_error
What went wrong on the last attempt (if anything).
suppressions & unsubscribe_tokens authoring Candidates who opted out, and the stable per-address token behind every unsubscribe link.
normalized_email
Opt-outs are keyed by address, so they survive deleting and re-importing a candidate.
suppressed_via
Link click, one-click header from the mail client, or a recruiter action.

Send admission & ledger — who fired, when

send_tokens audit A permanent record of every "go" signal the database has ever given. Never updated. The pacing math reads from here.
who & when
Which recruiter's mailbox was used, at what moment.
recipient
Which campaign_contact got the green light.
mailbox snapshot
The actual Gmail address in use at the time — preserved even if the recruiter later reconnects a different one.
send_attempts ledger One row per attempt to actually deliver. Starts as "started", ends as "sent", "failed", "retry_scheduled" or "send_unknown".
attempt number
1st try, 2nd try, etc., for this candidate.
status
started → sent / failed / retry / unknown.
timing
When the attempt began and when it finished.
outcome details
Gmail's message id on success, or a scrubbed error and failure category on failure.

OAuth — the mailbox keys

sender_credentials oauth Each recruiter's connected mailbox. The actual tokens live in Supabase Vault — this table just points to them.
provider & email
Today: always Google. Plus the actual Gmail address that was connected.
vault pointers
Locker numbers for the access & refresh tokens. The tokens themselves never sit on this row.
expiry
When the access token needs refreshing.
status
Active, needs-to-reconnect, or disabled. Losing "active" pauses that recruiter's live campaigns.
last error
Why the connection broke (with any token values scrubbed out).

Reputation — the warmup tracker

sender_reputation_scopes reputation One row per personal mailbox or per company domain. Knows how new it is and whether it's healthy enough to keep sending.
identity
A personal mailbox (gmail.com, outlook.com, …) or a whole company domain shared by the recruiting team.
state
Warming (new), cooling down (temporary brake), or safety paused (Gmail flagged something).
warmup_started_at
When this scope actually sent its first email. The whole tier table flows from this timestamp.
pause reason
If paused, why — visible to an operator.
sender_reputation_scope_audit audit Logs every time a human touched a reputation scope (e.g. cleared a safety pause).
action
What they did.
actor
Who did it.
before / after
Snapshot of the change.
scope_deferral_rollups counters "How often did the dispatcher skip a candidate, and why?" Aggregated per worker cycle, then drained into the worker's logs.
reason
Daily cap hit, cooldown active, sender too recent, safety paused.
count
How many skips of that kind this cycle.
